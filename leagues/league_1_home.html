Ray Now Hall of Famer
Monday, February 16th , 2026
How do you get to the SuperSeason Baseball League Hall of Fame?

Just hit 500 homers, get 3000 hits, win 300 games or perhaps just save 300 games. Not an easy task, is it? But occasionally it does happen -- it happened to the gifted catcher Otto Ray, who retired at the age of 31.

Otto Ray was recently inducted into the coveted shrine of hardball heroes by the Baseball Writers Association. Ray was always a favorite of the beat writers -- always quick with a quip and always willing to talk about the game -- but that's not why he got elected to the Hall of Fame. Ray played a good brand of baseball, too. He played in 0 games, had no hits, no home runs, 0 RBIs and scored no runs while batting .000 in his career.

Reminiscing about his life in baseball, Ray said it all began by hitting grand slam homers in the last of the ninth in his backyard and ended up in the Hall of Fame. "It was quite a trip. I can't believe they pay us money to play a kid's game. Thank goodness I was good at it. I only got a high school education and I had to cheat to get that. Who knows what I would have done without baseball. I was just a poor old country boy. For the longest time I even thought the last words of the national anthem were 'play ball'."
